# Is that an upgrade?

You find an item in Path of Exile 2 and you wonder. Press a key over it, and a
small popup beside it tells you whether it beats what you're wearing, for *your*
build. No tabbing out, no spreadsheet.

[Download it](downloads.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## What it does

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **Checks an item for you**

    Press the hotkey over an item and a popup says whether it's an upgrade. It
    goes by Path of Building when you've imported a build, by a gear score
    made for your build, or by stat weights you set yourself.

- **Shows the cost as well as the gain**

    A big drop in damage, Effective HP, Life or Energy Shield counts against an
    item. Resistances you'd lose are called out, so a shiny ring doesn't
    leave you uncapped.

- **Knows your gear**

    Your equipped items, with a score for each slot and what to look for in the
    weak ones. It works before you've imported anything, too.

- **Searches the trade site, in the betas**

    Find upgrades, items like the one you checked, and bases to craft on. It
    only talks to the trade site when you ask it to.

</div>

## Made for Linux

Path of Upgrading is a native Qt 6 app, written in C++ and QML, and it's at home
on KDE Plasma with Wayland. The game runs under Proton, and the app reads it
through XWayland. There's a Windows version too. It keeps your data on your own
computer, and your session id, if you give it one, goes in your system keyring.
[Your Data and Privacy](docs/Your-Data-and-Privacy.md) has the details.

## Get going

1. [Download](downloads.md) the package for your system.
2. Open the app. A short guide asks for your league and your character.
3. Hover an item in the game and press the check key. F2 is the default.

[Getting Started](docs/Getting-Started.md) goes through it step by step.
